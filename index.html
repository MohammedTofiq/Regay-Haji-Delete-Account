Account and Data Deletion Request

If you have created an account in the ڕێگەی حاجی (Regay Haji) mobile application and wish to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data, you can do so by submitting a request via email.

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Account Profile: Your full name, email address, and authentication credentials (including Google Sign-In profile identifiers).
  • User Preferences & Activity: Saved agency favorites, app settings, and interaction logs stored on our backend servers.

Data Retention Policy

Upon receiving your request, all personal data linked to your account will be permanently deleted from our active database within 7 business days. No retained records or personal logs will be kept.
